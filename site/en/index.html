About Miguel Anxo Bastos

Miguel Anxo Bastos Boubeta holds a PhD in Political Science and is an associate professor at the University of Santiago de Compostela. His academic research spans Austrian economic theory, the philosophy of law, and the critique of state interventionism.

Through his lectures and publications, he has contributed decisively to the dissemination of classical liberal thought across the Spanish-speaking world, with special focus on the history of political ideas, political economy, and the ethics of liberty.

His teaching, characterized by a rigorous yet accessible and humorous lecturing style, has reached tens of thousands of students and viewers worldwide. This volume collects them in written form.
